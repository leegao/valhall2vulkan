# ValhallSSA Bug Manifest

This manifest catalogues every closed and open equivalence divergence discovered during `tools.coverage` and `tools.verify.shader_equiv_verifier` sweeps. Keep this file updated whenever a bug is recorded into `bugs/*.spvasm` or closed by a fix.

## Status Legend

* **CLOSED**: Root-caused, fixed, and covered by a CTest regression test.
* **OPEN**: Active divergence with a verified repro (`.spvasm` in `bugs/` or sample/corpus shader) awaiting a fix.
